## What Forever Mini Reminder does

| | Feature | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| 🔔 | [**Smart Reminders**](Smart-Reminders) | Context-aware warnings for missing class effects, resources, timers, charges and preparation states. |
| ☠️ | [**Active DoTs**](Active-DoTs) | Supported DoTs grouped by enemy, with optional remaining-damage estimates. |
| 🎯 | [**Combat Indicators**](Combo-Points) | Combo points plus supported Charge, Intercept and Feral Charge nameplate cues. |
| 🧪 | [**Consumables**](Professions-and-Consumables) | Bag-aware reminders for weapon enhancements, elixirs, flasks, Well Fed foods and camping effects. |
| 🗺️ | [**World Map Guide**](World-Map-Guide) | Terrain reveal, level ranges, passages, transport routes, flight points and instance entrances/maps. |
| 🌦️ | [**Environment HUD**](Environment-HUD) | Realm time, weather information, modelled temperatures and a separate water/breath display. |
| 🎒 | [**Bag Bar**](Bag-Bar) | Free/total bag slots and Hunter ammunition counts directly on the game's bag buttons. |
| 🎮 | [**Mini Games & AFK**](Mini-Games-and-AFK) | Two taxi-flight mini games and decorative race-themed AFK scenes. |

The addon includes both **English and Turkish interface text**. Spell and item names follow the game client where appropriate.

## Quick setup

1. Install the folder as **`ForeverShamanReminder`** without renaming it.
2. In game, left-click the minimap button or enter **`/fsrsettings`**.
3. Enable the reminders and utilities you want for that character.
4. Choose **Large / Compact**, then use **Move / Lock** to place the displays.

Your class and consumable choices are character-specific. General appearance and utility settings are shared where documented.

---

## Choose how much you see

Forever Mini Reminder is designed around **context**, not constant noise. Preparation warnings can be hidden while mounted, in resting areas, or while dead/ghost. Active timers, DoTs, bag information and world utilities can remain visible when they are still relevant.

It does **not** provide rotation guidance, cast abilities automatically, or use items automatically.
